# The Bengaluru Clinic Weekly · Issue 01: The Practice of Mindful Rest

### Rest Is Not a Reward — It Is an Operating Requirement
In our fast-paced urban lives, we often treat rest as something we must earn after exhaustive exertion. Yet restorative sleep and intentional pauses are foundational to mental clarity, emotional regulation, and biological health.

### This Week's Everyday Practice: The Digital Sunset
- **Dim the Lights at 9:00 PM**: Melatonin production is sensitive to blue spectrum lighting. Lower the screen brightness and switch to warm, ambient lamps 60 minutes before retiring.
- **The 3-Minute Brain Dump**: Keep a discreet paper notebook beside your bed. Jot down tomorrow's to-do items to release your cognitive working memory before closing your eyes.
- **Sip Warm Water or Chamomile**: Give your nervous system a comforting thermal cue that the active day has concluded.
