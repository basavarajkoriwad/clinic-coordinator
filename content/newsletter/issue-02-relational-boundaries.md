# The Bengaluru Clinic Weekly · Issue 02: Relational Boundaries & Empathy

### Boundaries Are the Distance at Which I Can Love Both of Us
Healthy interpersonal relationships thrive not when boundaries are non-existent, but when they are explicit, respected, and rooted in mutual dignity.

### This Week's Everyday Practice: The "Pause Before Responding"
- **Notice the Somatic Surge**: When an interaction triggers tension, notice your breathing and heart rate before replying.
- **Differentiate Emotion from Action**: You can validate another person's feelings ("I understand why that felt frustrating") while upholding your own energetic limits ("I am unable to take that on tonight").
- **Express Needs Without Accusation**: Frame requests around your internal experience rather than the other person's shortcomings.
