# The Bengaluru Clinic Weekly · Issue 04: Navigating Life Transitions

### Change Is Inevitable; Growth Is Intentional
Major life transitions—whether career adjustments, relationship evolution, or personal milestones—naturally provoke feelings of disorientation.

### This Week's Everyday Practice: Micro-Routines
- **Anchor to One Consistent Morning Habit**: Drink one glass of water mindfully or step outdoors to catch five minutes of natural daylight.
- **Grant Yourself Permission to Be a Beginner**: Transition periods carry cognitive load. Lower expectations for perfection while routines stabilize.
- **Focus on the Controllable**: Divide your current concerns into things within your control and things outside it. Direct your energy strictly to the former.
