# The Bengaluru Clinic Weekly · Issue 03: Grounding Through Everyday Stress

### Regulating the Nervous System from the Bottom-Up
When deadlines and urban stimulation accumulate, our cognitive faculties can become hypervigilant. Calming the mind often begins with soothing the body.

### This Week's Everyday Practice: Box Breathing (Sama Vritti)
- **Inhale**: Slowly through your nose for 4 counts.
- **Hold**: Gently with lungs full for 4 counts.
- **Exhale**: Smoothly through your mouth for 4 counts.
- **Hold**: Rest at empty lungs for 4 counts.
Repeat 4 cycles. This activates the vagus nerve and downregulates the sympathetic fight-or-flight response within 90 seconds.
